End-to-End TCM Knowledge Graph System

Transforms knowledge from five TCM textbooks into a queryable, validated, and continuously improvable graph.

33,214 entities37,766 relations3,420+ herbal-tea relations

Constraint

Knowledge in TCM textbooks is distributed across unstructured text. One-pass LLM extraction creates entity drift, relation conflicts, and errors that are difficult to audit.

My actions

  • Built a pipeline combining embedding retrieval, a few-shot example bank, and LLM extraction.
  • Designed automatic scoring: low-confidence results are re-extracted, while high-confidence results enter the graph and improve the example bank.
  • Implemented normalized JSON/Excel ingestion, entity resolution, conflict detection, and relation reliability checks.

Outcome

The final graph contains 33,214 entities and 37,766 relations across diseases, syndrome patterns, formulas, and medicines, creating a reusable foundation for RAG and TCM LLM evaluation.
